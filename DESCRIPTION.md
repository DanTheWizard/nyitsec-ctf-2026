# Welcome to the NYITSec CTF!

This is a **hands-on, introductory Capture the Flag (CTF) event** where you can explore different cybersecurity topics, practice technical skills, and discover what interests you. Whether you are new to cybersecurity or already have experience, there is something to try.

## Choose your difficulty

- **Beginner:** Get comfortable with command-line tools, investigate authentication logs, decode messages, and try a fictional password audit.
- **Easy:** Investigate file types, hidden information, steganography, website source code, and a folder full of clues.
- **Intermediate:** Explore Git history, captured network traffic, DNS, suspicious Python code, and a deliberately vulnerable fictional website.

**New to Linux?** Try the optional, ungraded **Linux & CLI Quick Start** resources first.

## Points and leaderboards

Complete challenges to earn flags and build your score. A **custom event leaderboard** will track points and the challenges completed, with both an **overall ranking** and **separate rankings for Beginner, Easy, and Intermediate**. Use the challenge list to choose what to work on next.

## AI is welcome and encouraged

You **may use AI tools**, including ChatGPT, to learn commands, ask for explanations, develop ideas, debug scripts, and work through problems. Ask for hints or examples if you get stuck. Try to understand why a solution works rather than simply copying it. AI can make mistakes, so verify suggestions in the training environment.

Only work with the fictional files, accounts, and local services provided in these authorized challenges. Do not share real credentials, private keys, or other sensitive information with AI tools.

## How to participate

1. Open any challenge that interests you. You do **not** need to complete them in order.
2. Read the instructions and use the pwn.college Workspace to investigate.
3. When you have the answer, run `nyitsec-answer` in the terminal.
4. Submit the unique flag it prints on the challenge page to record your solve.

**You are not expected to finish every challenge during the event.** This CTF introduces some of the cybersecurity subjects NYITSec plans to cover more deeply in future club events. Explore, ask questions, and have fun learning!

**Workspace reminder:** Your challenge files are copied to `/nyitsec-ctf`, a temporary working folder. Save anything you want to keep under `~/` before restarting or switching challenges.
